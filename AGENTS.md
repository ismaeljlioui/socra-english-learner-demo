# Research working instructions

Read `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` before substantive research. Maintain `SOURCES.md` as the shared source and evidence log.

- The project is English-learner support for Socra's conversational assessments. Do not confuse English learners with the EL Education organization; the latter remains an unresolved reference from the supervisor's notes.
- Research should inform a technical product specification and standalone prototype, eventually a product capability. A research paper is not the intern's current deliverable.
- Explore independently of Socra's current codebase. Do not invent constraints, integrations, or internal architectures.
- Separate supervisor instructions, verified findings, vendor claims, interpretations, proposed designs, and unknowns.
- Prefer original research and official documentation. Read sources before making claims; record direct URLs and relevant page/section locators. A bibliography is not proof that a product implements or validates the cited practice.
- Critique evidence: population, setting, methods, comparator, outcome, confounders, limitations, and transfer to spoken assessment. Do not treat engagement, expert preference, or observational associations as proof of learning gains or causality.
- For each candidate support, examine language access, preservation of the assessed skill and rigor, answer leakage, fairness, and voice-input failure modes. Distinguish English proficiency from subject understanding and broad judgments of ability.
- Preserve uncertainty. Identify where educator review or empirical testing is needed; do not claim fairness or educational validity from a prototype alone.
- Use subagents for bounded independent research or critique when useful. Give them explicit questions and output boundaries, avoid concurrent edits to shared files, and verify and synthesize their findings.
- Keep documentation lean. Update these files as needed; do not create extra trackers, specifications, infrastructure, or code until the work calls for them.
- Weekly email updates are a future working arrangement. Do not schedule or send messages without an explicit request and established recipients.
